3. Should I choose HD (Analog) or IP CCTV cameras for my home, shop, or office?

For small shops and homes on a budget, HD (HDCVI/TVI) cameras offer clear 1080p/2.4MP video at an affordable price. For offices, warehouses, housing societies, and factories, IP PoE cameras are recommended because they deliver sharper zoom clarity (4MP–5MP), built-in audio, smart motion alerts, and single-cable CAT6 networking.

4. Can I watch my CCTV cameras live on my mobile phone from anywhere?

Yes. We configure mobile live view on both Android and iPhone (using apps like gCMOB / iCMOB for CP PLUS) so you can watch live feeds, play back past recordings, and receive instant motion alerts from anywhere in the world.

5. How many days of CCTV recording can be stored?

With H.265+ video compression, a 1TB surveillance hard disk stores roughly 15 to 20 days of continuous recording for 4 cameras, while a 2TB hard disk stores around 30 to 40 days before automatically overwriting the oldest footage.
